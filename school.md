## **What you get**

A funded intervention programme, built around your priorities. Delivered online, one to one, on our platform.

**One-to-one sessions.** Every session is a single pupil and a single specialist, for a full 60 minutes. No group dilution.

**Matched specialists.** We match each pupil to a specialist by subject, level, and need. Our specialists come from leading universities and teach across British, IB, and American curriculum.

**Progress you can see.** You get regular reporting on attendance, engagement, and progress across the cohort.
